Privacy Policy

JUMPjet for iPhone and iPad · last updated 27 August 2026

The short version. JUMPjet collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is reported to me or to anyone else.

What leaves your device, and when

JUMPjet runs its conformational sampling entirely on your device. The only time it uses the network is when you ask it to fetch a structure, and then it sends just the accession or PDB identifier you typed to the public archive that holds it:

ServiceWhat is sentOperated by
AlphaFold Protein Structure DatabaseThe UniProt accession you enteredEMBL-EBI and Google DeepMind
UniProtKBThe UniProt accession you enteredUniProt Consortium
PDBeThe accession, or the PDB entry identifier you enteredEMBL-EBI

Those requests carry no identifier of you, of your device or of your installation. They are ordinary anonymous HTTPS requests, and they are the same requests your web browser would make if you visited those archives yourself. Each of those organisations keeps its own server logs under its own privacy policy, which I do not control and cannot see.

If you never fetch a structure, JUMPjet makes no network requests at all.

What stays on your device

Downloaded structures, your run settings and any sampling results are stored only on your device, in the app's own container. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Photos

When you export a movie or a report card and choose to save it, JUMPjet asks for permission to add it to your photo library. That permission is add-only: the app cannot read your library, and it never does. If you decline, everything else still works, and you can still share the file through the standard share sheet instead.

Children

JUMPjet is a scientific tool with no user accounts, no messaging, no user-generated content and no advertising. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.
